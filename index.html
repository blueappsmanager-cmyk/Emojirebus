1. Overview & Commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how BlueAppsBox ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") manages and respects your privacy when you play our mobile game Emoji Rebus - Brain Test (Package Name: com.blueapps.rebusemojibrain).

We do not require account registration, we do not sell your personal data, and the game is designed to run safely on your Android device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

BlueAppsBox does not collect, track, or store any sensitive personal data:

  • No Personal Identifiers: No real names, postal addresses, phone numbers, or government IDs.
  • No User Accounts: No password, email, or social media logins required.
  • No Financial Info: We do not collect credit cards, banking info, or financial details.
  • No Media Scanning: No camera, photos, contacts, or microphone access.
  • No GPS Tracking: No precise physical location tracking.

3. Local Device Data Storage

All your game progression (solved levels up to 300, earned stars, hints activated, and sound settings) is stored purely locally on your device via HTML5 localStorage. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time via Android app settings.

4. Advertising: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Our application displays ads provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to support free gameplay. This includes Banner Ads, App Open Ads, and optional Rewarded Video Ads (which users can voluntarily watch to get hints, letter shuffle, level restart, or refill lives).

Google AdMob may process non-personally identifiable diagnostic device data (such as Google Advertising ID, device model, OS version, and ad interaction logs) in accordance with Google's policies: Google Advertising Policy.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Emoji Rebus - Brain Test is a family-friendly game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA).
