Overview: BlueAppsBox operates Moody Bubble Shooter (package: com.blueapps.bubblemoody) as a free, ad-supported casual mobile and online arcade game. We prioritize your privacy and data security. This Privacy Policy details how information is handled in strict compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR, and CCPA.

1. Information Collection & Usage

Moody Bubble Shooter is designed so that players can immediately enjoy arcade gameplay without mandatory user account registrations, personal email submissions, phone numbers, or passwords.

  • Device & Technical Identifiers: We and our verified third-party partners (Google Mobile Ads) automatically collect non-personal device information, including device model, operating system version, system language, network status, and screen resolution to render the game canvas accurately at 60 FPS.
  • Gameplay Statistics: Levels unlocked (1–300), star counts, high scores, shot accuracy, and speed timer durations are retained locally on your hardware to preserve game state across launches.
  • Advertising ID: Standard Google Advertising ID (GAID) is accessed by Google AdMob to deliver relevant and non-intrusive advertisements.

2. Local Data Storage & Cookies

The Game utilizes standard browser localStorage directly on your device rather than sending personal credentials to external servers. This local storage stores:

  • Your highest unlocked level (Levels 1 to 300).
  • Cumulative star ratings (1 to 3 stars per level).
  • Audio settings (Mute / Unmute preferences).
  • Level high scores and personal records.

You can clear this saved progress at any time by clearing the application cache/data in your Android Device Settings.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Services (Google AdMob)

To keep the Game 100% free for global players, Moody Bubble Shooter integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous data in compliance with Google's Privacy Policy:

  • Banner Ads: Displayed cleanly at the screen perimeter.
  • App Open Ads: Displayed on initial app launch or resume to prevent in-game interruption.
  • Rewarded Ads: User-initiated video advertisements where players voluntarily choose to view a sponsor message in exchange for in-game revives (+15 seconds bonus time, +5 extra bubbles, and pushing the descending ceiling back up).

For more details on Google's advertising data practices, visit: Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Children's Privacy Protection (COPPA & Families Policy)

Our game is built for family-friendly arcade entertainment. We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy requirements:

  • We do not knowingly collect, use, or disclose personal information from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
  • AdMob SDK is configured to serve age-appropriate, non-targeted ads in compliance with COPPA guidelines.
  • If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has provided unauthorized data, please contact us immediately at blueappsmanager@gmail.com to wipe all associated records.

5. Data Retention, Security & Zero-Sale Policy

Zero Sale of Personal Data: BlueAppsBox does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any player data, personal credentials, or usage logs to third parties or data brokers.

All in-app communications with online content delivery networks utilize robust Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) encryption protocols to prevent unauthorized interception.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Regardless of your geographic location, you possess comprehensive rights over your digital footprint:

  • Right to Know & Access: You can request verification of any data processed in connection with your device.
  • Right to Deletion: You can request immediate erasure of analytical telemetry.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising: You can reset or disable your Google Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings > Google > Ads > Delete Advertising ID.

7. Policy Changes & Updates

BlueAppsBox reserves the right to periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect evolving game features or regulatory amendments. Any revisions will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
